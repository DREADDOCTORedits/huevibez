About

Music for the in-between hours. Huevibez makes Afro-soul and Afro-jazz textures loosened with pop hooks, written and recorded from the coast in Mombasa, Kenya. Off the mic, Muhidini directs and shoots the visuals himself — treating each song and each frame as the same story, told twice.
